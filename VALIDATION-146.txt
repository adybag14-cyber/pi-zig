Archive, patch and transfer gates
---------------------------------
The published source ZIP is required to have one checkpoint-146 root and no
.git, .zig-cache or zig-out entries. Its exact extracted bytes are validated
with formatting, source audit, Node syntax, the complete test graph, all three
Debug builds and executable smokes.

The checkpoint-145 to checkpoint-146 patch is applied to a pristine extraction
of the uploaded baseline. The reconstruction is required to match the packaged
checkpoint-146 source byte-for-byte and pass the same source/build gates.

Every published artifact is copied in forced 1 MiB chunks to its final
/mnt/data path, flushed and fsynced, read completely, compared byte-for-byte,
independently SHA-256 hashed, checked through three stable size/mtime/hash
snapshots and verified against the final checksum manifest before the response
containing the download links is sent.
