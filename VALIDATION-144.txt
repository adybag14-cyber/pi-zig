Transfer-completion protocol
----------------------------
After all reports are frozen, every linked artifact is read completely, copied in
forced 1 MiB chunks with flush/fsync, compared byte-for-byte, independently
SHA-256 hashed, and polled through three stable size/mtime/hash snapshots. The
checksum manifest is then verified. Final hashes live in the external manifest so
this source report does not require a recursive self-hash.
